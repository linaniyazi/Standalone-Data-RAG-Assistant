FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS: Order & Payment Process

Q: How long does it take to process a new order?
A: Standard orders are processed within 2-3 business days. Bulk orders
   over $5,000 may take up to 5 business days due to additional
   approval steps.

Q: What payment methods are accepted?
A: We accept bank transfer, credit card, and digital wallet payments.
   Cash payments are only accepted for in-store purchases.

Q: Can I apply a discount to an existing order?
A: Discounts can only be applied before an order is finalized. Once an
   order is confirmed, any discount request must go through the
   customer service team and may require manager approval depending on
   the discount percentage.

Q: What happens if a payment is late?
A: Payments not received by the due date incur a 2% monthly penalty,
   applied to the outstanding balance until payment is received.

Q: How can I check the status of my order?
A: Order status can be checked through the customer portal using your
   Order Reference number, or by contacting the regional support team.
