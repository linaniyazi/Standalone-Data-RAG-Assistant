MONTHLY PERFORMANCE REPORT
Period: October 2024
Prepared by: Sales Analytics Team

Executive Summary:
Total sales for October 2024 reached $59,687.75, with total profit of
$16,243.14, representing a profit margin of approximately 27.2%, the
strongest monthly margin recorded this year.

Regional Breakdown:
- West Region: Continued to lead in both sales and profit margin,
  driven primarily by strong Technology category performance.
- Central Region: Remained the weakest-performing region, consistent
  with prior quarters, due to lower margins across Furniture products.

Category Notes:
- Technology and Office Supplies categories maintained healthy margins
  above 15%.
- Furniture category continued to underperform, with the Tables
  sub-category showing a negative margin for the fourth consecutive
  month.

Recommendations:
1. Review pricing strategy for the Tables sub-category.
2. Consider reducing discount approval thresholds for Furniture orders
   given ongoing margin pressure.
3. Investigate replicating West region's Technology sales approach in
   underperforming regions.
