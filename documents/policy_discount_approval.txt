POLICY DOCUMENT: Discount Approval Policy
Document ID: POL-SALES-004
Effective Date: January 1, 2024
Department: Sales Operations

Purpose:
This policy defines the approval thresholds for discounts applied to
customer orders across all regions (West, East, South, Central).

Policy Details:

1. Discounts under 10%: Can be approved directly by the sales
   representative handling the order, no further authorization needed.

2. Discounts between 10% and 20%: Require approval from the Regional
   Sales Manager before being applied to the order.

3. Discounts above 20%: Require written approval from the Director of
   Sales, and must be documented with a business justification.

4. Furniture category orders: Due to historically low profit margins
   in this category (particularly Tables and Bookcases), any discount
   above 5% requires Regional Sales Manager approval regardless of the
   order size.

5. All approved discounts must be logged in the order management
   system with the approver's name and the reason for the discount.

This policy was last reviewed in October 2024 following a profitability
review of the Furniture category.
